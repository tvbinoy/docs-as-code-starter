Daymark is a simple task planner for people who want to keep their commitments in one place. Capture a task, give it a home, and decide when to work on it.

:::info About this site

Daymark is a **fictional product**. This help centre is an original documentation sample; the screens are illustrations, and there is no application to sign in to.

:::

## Start with one small project

Imagine you are preparing a website refresh. You need to review the homepage, collect feedback, and check the final links. Daymark gives each piece of work its own task.

![Illustrated Website refresh board with tasks in To do, In progress, and Done.](/img/daymark-board.svg)

*Example workspace: Website refresh. All names and tasks are sample content.*

## Find the right place

| Area | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbox** | Capture tasks before assigning them to a project. |
| **Today** | Find unfinished tasks due today or overdue. |
| **Upcoming** | See unfinished tasks with a future due date. |
| **Projects** | Group related tasks and follow their progress. |

Tasks without a due date stay available in their project or Inbox. They do not appear in Today or Upcoming.

## A simple way to work

1. **Capture** the next action as a task.
2. **Organize** it in a project.
3. **Plan** a realistic due date, if it needs one.
4. **Finish** it by setting its status to **Done**.

:::tip Keep the next action small

“Review the homepage heading” is easier to start than “Finish the website.”

:::
